Built around the catalog, not the download

Aniyaa finds listings. Your torrent app does the rest.

search

Search that feels native

Type a query or tap Show latest. Category chips sit under the bar so you never leave the list.

tune

Filters in one sheet

Trusted, no remakes, sort order, and subcategories. A dot on the filter button means extras are on.

swipe

Swipe for the action

Swipe a result right for magnet, left to bookmark. Open a listing for files, comments, and share.

link

Open from the browser

nyaa.si and sukebei listing or search links can open directly in Aniyaa.

bookmark

Bookmarks stay local

Star a listing and find it later. Swipe a bookmark left to remove it. Updates never wipe them.

history

Search history

Recent queries live on the History tab so you can jump back into a hunt without retyping.

notifications

Saved search alerts

Pin a search and Aniyaa can notify you when new results appear. Permission is asked first.

backup

Export and reset

Take a backup from Settings, or wipe local data. Nothing is stored on a server you do not control.

lock

App lock

PIN or biometrics before the catalog opens. Useful if the phone is shared or unlocked in public.

visibility_off

Hide the screen

Block screenshots and hide Aniyaa from recents when you want the listing list off the record.

palette

Material You themes

Wallpaper colors on Android 12+, extra palettes, and light or dark. The catalog should feel like your phone.

18_up_rating

Sukebei stays off

Adult catalog is optional and disabled until you turn it on in Settings if you are 18 or older.

Four steps, then you are browsing

No account. No extra runtime. Install and search.

Install the APK

Download the latest build and allow your browser or Files app to install it.

Search or show latest

Use the search bar, tap a category, or load the newest listings.

Open a listing

Read the description, file list, and comments. Copy or share if you need to.

Hand off the magnet

Aniyaa opens your torrent app. LibreTorrent, Flud, qBittorrent, or whichever you picked in Settings.

What Aniyaa is not

Keeping the scope small is the point.

public_off

Not the website

It is an unofficial client. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by nyaa.si or sukebei.nyaa.si.

file_download_off

Not a torrent engine

Aniyaa does not download files. It finds listings and passes a magnet or .torrent to another app.

cloud_off

Not a cloud account

Bookmarks, history, and saved searches stay on the device. There is nothing to sign in to.
